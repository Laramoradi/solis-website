Privacy Policy

Who we are

SOLIS Couture, Vienna, Austria, is responsible for the processing of your data on this website. You can reach us at contact@soliscouture.com.

What we collect

When you sign up to be notified, we store your email address, the date and time of your sign-up and where you signed up (this website). When you visit the website, the hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address in server logs. This is necessary to deliver the page.

Why we use it

We use your email address to send you a welcome email and to let you know about the SOLIS collection and its launch, including early access. We do not use it for anything else and we do not sell it.

Legal basis

We process your email address based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Sending emails for marketing purposes also requires your prior consent under Section 174 of the Austrian Telecommunications Act. You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future.

Who processes your data for us

  • Google (Google Sheets and Apps Script) stores the sign-up list and receives your email address when you sign up.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) sends our emails. Brevo records whether an email was delivered and opened and which links were clicked.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and Cloudflare manages the domain name.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces of this website. Your browser loads them from Google servers, so Google receives your IP address.

Where data is transferred outside the European Union, this is based on an adequacy decision of the European Commission or on standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you withdraw your consent. If you unsubscribe, we mark your address as unsubscribed so that we do not contact you again. On request, we delete it completely.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To withdraw your consent, use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every email or write to us. If you believe that the processing of your data violates data protection law, you can lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).
